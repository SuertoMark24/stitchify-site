Reviewer photos go here.

Name them however you like and point the <img src> in index.html at them, e.g.:
    assets/reviews/maria.jpg   ->   <img src="assets/reviews/maria.jpg" alt="" ... />

Notes:
- Square images work best (they're cropped to a circle, 52x52 displayed).
  Around 200x200 is plenty; larger just wastes bandwidth.
- If a reviewer has no photo, delete the <img> tag from their card and the
  coloured circle with their initial shows instead.
