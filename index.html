Risks, Liability & Terms of Use

Read this before you connect a real TikTok account. Plain language, no fine print games.

What Stitchify Pro actually is

Stitchify Pro is a self-hosted app that runs on your own PC. It automates a real browser to log into and post from your TikTok account(s), and it connects to third-party AI providers (Gemini, ElevenLabs, Higgsfield, MuAPI, etc.) using API keys you provide and pay for yourself. It is not a TikTok product and has no affiliation with TikTok.

TikTok's Terms of Service — the real risk

TikTok's Terms of Service may prohibit automated, bot-driven, or browser-automation-based posting. Using Stitchify Pro to post, schedule, or manage your account(s) can result in those accounts being flagged, rate-limited, shadow-restricted, or permanently banned by TikTok — with no warning and no appeal guaranteed. This can happen to a brand-new account or one you've had for years. There is no version of this software, or any automation tool like it, that can promise TikTok won't take action.

You are solely responsible for your accounts

You own and operate every TikTok account, and every AI-provider account/API key, that you connect to Stitchify Pro. You decide what gets posted, how often, and to which accounts. You are fully and solely liable for the consequences — including but not limited to account bans, restrictions, strikes, loss of followers, loss of monetization/rewards eligibility, or any action TikTok or any third-party service takes against you. The author(s) of Stitchify Pro accept no responsibility or liability for actions taken by TikTok, by any AI/API provider, or by any other third party as a result of you using this software.

No guarantees, no warranty

TikTok can change its website, app, or detection systems at any time without notice — this can break posting, scheduling, or any other automated feature without warning. Stitchify Pro is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including no guarantee of uptime, continued compatibility with TikTok, or that your accounts will avoid detection or action.

Rewards & earnings figures aren't guaranteed income
