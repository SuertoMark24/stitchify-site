Why I built this

Welcome — I built this for my own accounts first.

I'm a TikTok Shop affiliate, not a software company. I was running content across several accounts — motor parts, gadgets, home stuff — and losing entire evenings to the same loop: stitch the clips, write the caption, log into one account, upload, wait, log into the next. Making the content took minutes. Posting it took hours.

So I built Stitchify Pro for myself, to make that part disappear. Now the clips get made, queued, and posted to every account on a schedule I set once. I still decide what goes out — I just don't sit there doing it by hand any more.

Everything in here exists because I needed it on my own accounts first.

Risks, Liability & Terms of Use

Read this before you connect a real TikTok account. Plain language, no fine print games.

What Stitchify Pro actually is

Stitchify Pro is a self-hosted app that runs on your own PC. It automates a real browser to log into and post from your TikTok account(s), and it connects to third-party AI providers (Gemini, ElevenLabs, Higgsfield, MuAPI, etc.) using API keys you provide and pay for yourself. It is not a TikTok product and has no affiliation with TikTok.

TikTok's Terms of Service — the real risk

TikTok's Terms of Service may prohibit automated, bot-driven, or browser-automation-based posting. Using Stitchify Pro to post, schedule, or manage your account(s) can result in those accounts being flagged, rate-limited, shadow-restricted, or permanently banned by TikTok — with no warning and no appeal guaranteed. This can happen to a brand-new account or one you've had for years. There is no version of this software, or any automation tool like it, that can promise TikTok won't take action.

You are solely responsible for your accounts

You are fully and solely liable for the consequences — including but not limited to account bans, restrictions, strikes, loss of followers, loss of monetization/rewards eligibility, or any action TikTok or any third-party service takes against you. The author(s) of Stitchify Pro accept no responsibility or liability for actions taken by TikTok, by any AI/API provider, or by any other third party as a result of you using this software.

No guarantees, no warranty

Stitchify Pro is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including no guarantee of uptime, continued compatibility with TikTok, or that your accounts will avoid detection or action.
